Softub Whirlpool – Wiring Diagram

TH Elite switches pump · ozonator · Isonic valve · Tuya W218 water analysis

230V AC / 50Hz
Phase L (switched)
Neutral N
Protective earth PE
Ozonator (switched via TH Elite)
Isonic valve (12V via PSU, switched via TH Elite)
Tuya W218 (direct terminal tap)
✔ TH Elite switches pump, ozonator, and Isonic valve together — all power on/off with the temperature relay.
✔ Tuya W218 is connected directly to the terminal block (own 230V line) – runs permanently, independent of TH Elite.
✔ The W218's pH/ORP/TDS probes go into the pool water · the temperature probe monitors the electronics cabinet temperature.
⚠ Ozone only flows when the TH Elite relay is closed (pump running + ozonator active + Isonic valve open).
